## About

The Hi-Cycles lab investigates biogeochemical cycles in the ocean, with a focus on
the processes that govern oxygen and carbon exchange between the ocean and atmosphere.

Our work spans multiple scales — from improving ocean sensors and understanding
biological and physical mechanisms at the process level, to quantifying regional and
global air-sea fluxes.
